What this is

Two voices talking through the material of the lecture and the lab. The script was written by a language model from the course material (slides, speaker notes, lab notebooks) and read by a text-to-speech engine running locally on our own machine. These are synthetic voices: now and then they sound odd, misspeak, or put something imprecisely. Every episode exists in Czech and in English.

The podcast replaces neither the lecture nor the lab. It is a companion for the commute and for revision. Formulas, figures, derivations and code do not fit into audio — they are in the slides and in the notebook. What is taught and what is examined is set by the lecture and the lab, not by the podcast.
